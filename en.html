The place

Cocorná, a sanctuary of water

In eastern Antioquia, between 450 and 2,350 meters above sea level, Cocorná is a valley of crystal-clear rivers, waterfalls and humid forest. It was the land of the Tahamí and Pantágora peoples, and today it is a town that chose peace and rural tourism.

ClimateMild, around 23 °C (73 °F). It rains often: bring a rain jacket.
WaterThe Cocorná, Melcocho and Santo Domingo rivers, streams and waterfalls.
FlavorsPanela (unrefined cane sugar), cacao, coffee and country cooking.
CultureThe Panela Festival and the chocolate and trova music festival.
Why "Cocorná"?There are two versions. One says it comes from the Indigenous expression Pantagora Cocozna, "land of animals". The other, from local tradition, tells of a chief named Cocorná who led these lands when the Spanish arrived. Your guide tells you both.

Your trip in three moments

Before, during and after

What we ask of you at each stage so the trip is safe and leaves a good mark on Cocorná.

Before the trip

  • Sign up and fill in the health, allergy and mobility form.
  • Read and sign the contract; if a minor is traveling, send the written authorization from their parent or guardian.
  • Check the weather forecast (IDEAM, Colombia's weather service) and the packing list.
  • Keep your ID and health insurance card at hand.

2

During the tour

  • Follow the guide's instructions; enter the river only when the guide says so, and never alone.
  • Stay with the group on trails and transfers.
  • Keep a respectful silence in the museum and in places of memory.
  • No alcohol or drugs; respect everyone's personal space.
  • Zero tolerance for the commercial sexual exploitation of children and adolescents (Colombian Law 1336 of 2009).

Rubric 5 and 6 · Circumstances and support groups
Your safety

How we look after you

We plan ahead for what can happen at the river and in the mountains, so all you have to do is rest.

River with a group limit

We check the IDEAM forecast and flood alerts before leaving. If there is any risk, we do not go in the water.

Safe footing

We enter the water one at a time, with support, and show you how to walk safely.

Clear trails

We walk on known routes and carry repellent and a first aid kit.

Warmth after the river

Limited time in the water, dry clothes, a thermal blanket and a hot drink.

Emotional support

No one is forced to do anything. If something overwhelms you, we pause and breathe together.

☂

Plan B always ready

If it rains hard or a transfer fails, we have an indoor space and backup transport.

Group agreements

We count the group when leaving and arriving on every transfer A single entry and exit point at the river Maximum 10 people per trip and groups of 5 in the river You never go into the water alone Respectful silence in the museum and in places of memory We do not touch wildlife or pick plants Your waste leaves with you

Rubric 1 · Guide profile
Field photo · Juan David in Cocorná
Your guide

Juan David Gómez

"Like the river, we too need to flow, cleanse ourselves and begin again."

For years I traveled and taught yoga outside Colombia. I learned that the body, which is mostly water, behaves like a river: if it does not flow or rest, it loses its balance.

I came back and found in Cocorná a place that speaks that language. I trained as a tourism guide at SENA, Colombia's national training service, to lead groups safely and with respect for the people who live there.

I do not promise cures: I offer you the river, the mountains, local food and the true story of the land.

Terms of service

Clear from the very start

Before traveling you sign a simple contract with these terms. That way you, the guide and the hosts are all protected.

You book your spot with 50% of the price and pay the remaining 50% three days before departure. You receive a receipt and confirmation by WhatsApp.

If you cancel more than 7 days in advance, we refund 90%. Between 3 and 7 days, 50%. With less than 72 hours there is no refund, but you can transfer your spot to someone else.

If there is a flood alert or heavy rain, we do not go into the river: we switch to indoor yoga and breathing, or reschedule at no cost.

Before the trip you fill in a health form (allergies, medication, mobility). No practice is mandatory. Wellness activities do not replace medical treatment.

Follow the guide's instructions, leave no waste and take no plants or stones, no alcohol or drugs during the tour, and respect everyone's personal space.

Travelers must be 16 or older. Under-18s need written authorization from their parents or guardians and travel with a responsible adult. We reject the commercial sexual exploitation of children and adolescents (Colombian Law 1336 of 2009).

The price includes travel assistance insurance during the tour. Bring your health insurance card or travel insurance: it covers anything the assistance insurance does not.

During the tour, talk to your guide, who will listen in private and look for a solution right away. Afterwards, write to us through the WhatsApp group or the survey: we reply in writing within 5 business days.

We use your health and contact details only to provide the service and do not share them, except as needed in an emergency (Colombian Law 1581 of 2012). Photos of you are used only with your written permission.

The service is described in writing in the contract, includes assistance insurance and follows the Colombian guiding standard NTC 6507. If you have a complaint, we handle it, and you can also go to the municipal ombudsman (Personería).
